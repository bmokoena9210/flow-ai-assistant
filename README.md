# Flow AI Assistant

Build a modern, polished SaaS web app called Flow AI Workplace Productivity Assistant. Use a black background and touches of neon babyblue and white.

Create a fully responsive professional dashboard with a left sidebar and these 5 AI tools:

AI Email Generator — generate professional emails based on audience, purpose, tone and context.

Meeting Summarizer — turn meeting notes into summaries, key points, decisions, action items and deadlines.

AI Task Planner — enter tasks and have AI prioritize them and create a practical schedule.

AI Research Assistant — generate structured business insights, summaries and recommendations from a research topic.

AI Workplace Chat — conversational AI assistant for everyday work.

Also include Dashboard, Activity History and Settings.

Dashboard

Show a professional overview with productivity statistics, recent activity and quick-action cards for the 5 AI tools.

UX

Every AI tool should have a clear input → generate → loading → result workflow. Results should be editable and have copy/regenerate actions. Use realistic demo data so the app looks complete immediately.

Design

Use a premium modern SaaS aesthetic: clean, minimal, professional, slightly futuristic, excellent spacing, rounded cards, subtle borders/shadows, modern typography and smooth micro-interactions. Avoid excessive gradients or flashy graphics.

Make the sidebar, cards, forms and AI result panels reusable and consistent. Ensure excellent desktop, tablet and mobile responsiveness.

Responsible AI

Include the message:
“AI-generated content may require human review.”

Make the application feel like a real portfolio-ready AI SaaS MVP, not a static mockup. Prioritize polished UI, functional interactions, intuitive navigation and a cohesive user experience.
